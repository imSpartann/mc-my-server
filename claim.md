# Land Claim System

Protect your land and keep other players from building or breaking blocks in your claim.

## Watch the Tutorial

Watch the tutorial before creating your first claim:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ix-YRJK29dw

---

## Creating a Claim

1. Run `/claim wand` to receive a Claim Wand.
2. **Left-click** a block to set **Position 1**.
3. **Right-click** another block to set **Position 2**.
4. Run `/claim confirm` to create your claim.
5. Your land is now protected.

### Selection Commands

- `/claim wand` — Receive a Claim Wand.
- `/claim confirm` — Create your claim.
- `/claim cancel` — Cancel the current selection.

---

## Managing Claims

- `/claim info` — View information about the claim you are standing in.
- `/claim list` — View all of your claims.
- `/claim stats` — View your claim usage and limits.
- `/claim unclaim` — Remove the claim you are standing in.
- `/claim unclaim all` — Remove all of your claims.

---

## Trusting Players

You can allow other players to build and interact in your claim.

- `/claim trust <player>` — Give a player access to your claim.
- `/claim untrust <player>` — Remove a player's access.
- `/claim trusted` — View players who have access to your claim.

**Tip:** Only trust players you know and trust.

---

## Claim Protection

- `/claim protect on` — Enable claim protection.
- `/claim protect off` — Disable claim protection.
- `/claim mark` — Show your claim boundaries with particles.

Use `/claim mark` to make sure your claim covers the area you intended to protect.

---

## Claim Marketplace

You can sell and buy claims through the Claim Marketplace.

- `/claim market` — Open the Claim Marketplace.
- `/claim sell <price>` — List your claim for sale.
- `/claim buy` — Purchase a listed claim.
- `/claim unsell` — Remove your claim from the marketplace.
- `/claim market setspawn` — Set the marketplace teleport location.
- `/claim market clearspawn` — Remove the marketplace teleport location.

## Tips

- Claim your base as early as possible.
- Only trust players you know.
- Use `/claim mark` to verify your claim boundaries.
- Use `/claim stats` to monitor your remaining claim blocks.
