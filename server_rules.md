:scroll: **Server Rules Summary**

### :evergreen_tree: Old World

* PvP disabled
* No griefing
* No stealing
* No raiding
* Claims available in Old World, Old World Nether, and Old World End
* Unclaimed builds are at your own risk
* Unclaimed regions are not protected or supported by staff
* Grave trapping prohibited

### :crossed_swords: New World

* PvP enabled
* Claims available in New World and New World Nether
* Claims disabled in New World End
* Unclaimed builds are at your own risk
* Unclaimed regions are not protected or supported by staff
* Grave trapping prohibited

### :pick: Resource World

* PvP enabled
* Lifesteal enabled
* Claims disabled
* No graves
* Items drop normally on death
* High-risk environment
* World may reset periodically
* Do not build permanent bases here
* Builds and items in the Resource World are not protected or supported by staff

### :shield: Claim Protection

Claims protect against:

* Griefing
* Chest theft
* TNT damage
* Lava & water griefing
* Creeper damage

**Important:** Build inside a claim if you want protection. Unclaimed builds and regions are the player's responsibility. Staff will not restore, protect, or compensate for damage, theft, or loss occurring in unclaimed regions.

### :package: Item Restoration

* Item restoration is **not supported** for normal gameplay-related losses.
* This includes items lost due to death, dropping items, player mistakes, or other player-related incidents.
* Item restoration may only be considered when the loss is caused by a **confirmed server bug**.

### :wrench: Farm Requirements

Please make sure your farms properly contain their mobs.

When mobs escape a farm and wander around outside the intended area, they continue to exist with their AI active. If enough mobs accumulate, this can create unnecessary server load and affect overall server performance.

**Requirements:**

* Mobs must remain properly contained within the farm.
* Do not allow mobs to escape the farm and wander around nearby areas.
* Farms must not unnecessarily accumulate mobs.
* AFK farming is allowed, but the farm must still meet these requirements.
* If a farm is causing performance issues, staff may require it to be fixed, modified, reduced, or disabled.

These requirements are intended to keep farms profitable while preventing unnecessary entity load on the server.
