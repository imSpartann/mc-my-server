# My-SMP Plus Shop

The Plus Shop uses **PlayerPoints** as its currency. Players can spend their points on temporary Plus Tools, permanent Netherite equipment, and other weapons.

## Plus Tools vs. Netherite Tools

**Plus Tools** have special abilities beyond vanilla Minecraft mechanics but expire 24 hours after their first use. Once the timer expires, the tool is automatically removed.

**Netherite Tools** are permanent, fully enchanted vanilla-equivalent tools. They do not expire.

## How to Earn PlayerPoints

Players can earn PlayerPoints through the following activities:

1. **Trivia Challenges** — New challenges appear every 15 minutes. Only the first, second, and third players to answer correctly receive rewards.
2. **Player Kills** — Earn 10 PlayerPoints for killing another player, either in or outside duels. A cooldown prevents players from repeatedly farming kills using alternate accounts.
3. **Playtime** — Earn 1 PlayerPoint for every 10 minutes of playtime.

## Important: Buying Plus Tools from the Auction House

**Check whether a Plus Tool has already been used before purchasing it from the Auction House.**

The 24-hour timer starts on the tool's first use, not when you purchase it. If the previous owner has already activated it, the remaining time may be less than 24 hours.

Expired Plus Tools are automatically removed. Purchase used tools at your own discretion.

## Why Use the Plus Shop?

The Plus Shop provides more ways to spend PlayerPoints beyond purchasing spawners. Players can choose between temporary tools with special abilities and permanent equipment for everyday use.

Plus Tools offer abilities beyond standard vanilla mechanics, such as:

- **Plus Shovel** — Breaks up to 9 blocks in a single use.
- **Plus Sell Axe** — Sells items directly from a chest.

Permanent Netherite Tools, on the other hand, retain their vanilla-equivalent functionality with their specified enchantments.

The 24-hour limit on Plus Tools helps maintain server balance while allowing players to access their special abilities.
