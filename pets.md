# Pets Guide

There are two ways to get a pet:

1. **Buy a pet from the Pet Shop in the Lobby**
2. **Tame a mob yourself**

---

## Buy a Pet

You can purchase pets from the **Pet Shop in the Lobby**.

Visit the Lobby and interact with the **Pet Shop** to see the available pets and purchase them.

---

## Tame a Pet

You can also turn certain mobs into pets yourself.

The taming method depends on the mob.

### Most Pets

1. Find the mob.
2. Attack it until it has **very low HP**.
3. Put a **Lead** on it.
4. Give it the required **food**.
5. The mob becomes your pet.

### Pets That Need Normal Taming

Some mobs must be tamed normally first.

1. Tame the mob using the normal Minecraft method.
2. Put a **Lead** on it.
3. Give it the required food.
4. The mob becomes your pet.

**Examples:**

- **Horse** → Ride it until hearts appear.
- **Wolf** → Tame it with Bones.
- **Cat** → Tame it with Raw Cod.

### Mobs You Create

For mobs that are created instead of found naturally:

1. Create the mob normally.
2. Put a **Lead** on it.
3. Give it the required food, if applicable.
4. The mob becomes your pet.

**Example:** Iron Golem.

---

## Mobs That Cannot Be Pets

These mobs cannot be turned into pets:

- Ender Dragon
- Ghast
- Wither

# Quick Examples

### Buy a Pet

**Lobby → Pet Shop → Choose a Pet → Purchase**

### Make a Zombie Your Pet

**Zombie → Low HP → Lead → Rotten Flesh → Pet**

### Make a Horse Your Pet

**Horse → Tame normally → Lead → Sugar/Wheat/Apple → Pet**

---

## Important

- Pets can be **purchased from the Pet Shop in the Lobby**.
- Some pets can also be **tamed yourself**.
- Check the pet list for the required food.
- When taming a mob yourself, use a **Lead** before feeding it.
