<h1>Teams guide:</h1>
<h2>Why create a team?</h2>

Teams are a way of creating a a great community between other players and you, creating allies, making opportunities possible with teams, progress and teamwork.

<h2>How to create one?</h2> 

Start by typing "/team create **name**" But they come with a cost of 1 million per making one, spend your money wisely and save up to create your very first own team, 

<h2>What if I don't have enough money?</h2>

Get enough gear and start killing the Ender Dragons and Wardens each kill of one of these mobs grants you 100k, kill 10 of these mobs and you get yourself 1 million!, Or if you are not the type to grind, join the available teams!, each teams player's capacity is 10 player per team (**"ask the owner of the team first"**) if you would like to join theirs.
