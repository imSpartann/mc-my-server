## 📦 How to Craft and Unlock Minions

---

### 🧑‍🌾 Farmer Minion

**Crafting Recipe:**

* 8x Wheat
* 1x Wooden Hoe (center)

**Unlock Requirement:**

* Break 250 wheat
* **Reward:** Unlocks Farmer Minion placement

**Minion Tool Required to Start:**

* Hoe

---

### ⛏️ Miner Minion

**Crafting Recipe:**

* 8x Cobblestone
* 1x Wooden Pickaxe (center)

**Unlock Requirement:**

* Place 250 stone blocks
* **Reward:** Unlocks Miner Minion placement

**Minion Tool Required to Start:**

* Pickaxe

> 💡 *Tip for OneBlock:* The Miner Minion is useful for automatically mining stone and other blocks generated as you progress through your OneBlock island.

---

### 🪓 Lumber Minion

**Crafting Recipe:**

* 8x Oak Logs
* 1x Wooden Axe (center)

**Unlock Requirement:**

* Break 250 wood blocks
* **Reward:** $6000, 3 Starter Crate Keys, unlocks Lumber Minion placement

**Minion Tool Required to Start:**

* Axe

---

### 🎣 Fisher Minion

**Crafting Recipe:**

* 8x Raw Cod
* 1x Fishing Rod (center)

**Unlock Requirement:**

* Catch 250 fish
* **Reward:** $6000, 5 Starter Crate Keys, unlocks Fisher Minion placement

**Minion Tool Required to Start:**

* Fishing Rod

---

### 💰 Seller Minion

**Crafting Recipe:**

* 8x Emeralds
* 1x Chest (center)

**Unlock Requirement:**

* Pick up 250 items
* **Reward:** $7000, 4 Starter Crate Keys, unlocks Seller Minion placement

**Minion Tool Required to Start:**

* Bow

---

### 🗡️ Slayer Minion

**Crafting Recipe:**

* 8x Rotten Flesh
* 1x Wooden Sword (center)

**Unlock Requirement:**

* Kill 250 zombies
* **Reward:** $20000, 30 Starter Crate Keys, unlocks Slayer Minion placement

**Minion Tool Required to Start:**

* Sword

---

## ⚠️ Important Note

You must complete the unlock requirement **before** you can place each minion.

Crafting is available anytime, but **placement requires completing its challenge**.
